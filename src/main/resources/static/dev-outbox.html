💡 Want real emails delivered directly to your Gmail inbox?

You can configure real SMTP delivery at any time by adding these environment variables in your Render dashboard: 

SPRING_MAIL_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SPRING_MAIL_PORT=587
SPRING_MAIL_USERNAME=your-email@gmail.com
SPRING_MAIL_PASSWORD=your-gmail-16-char-app-password
SPRING_MAIL_PROPERTIES_MAIL_SMTP_AUTH=true
SPRING_MAIL_PROPERTIES_MAIL_SMTP_STARTTLS_ENABLE=true
