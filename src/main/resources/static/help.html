Step-by-Step Guides

1

How to Create & Activate Your Account

  1. Go to Create account and enter your name, email, and password.
  2. On the Check Email screen, look for the green button: [👉 Click Here to Activate Account Instantly].
  3. If testing across tabs, open the Simulated Dev Outbox and click Verify & Activate.
  4. Once activated, you can sign in with your credentials right away!
Try Registering
2

How Adaptive Sign-In Works

  1. Enter your email and password on the Log in screen.
  2. Low Risk: Signing in from a known, trusted device logs you in immediately without hurdles.
  3. Medium Risk: If you use a new browser or location, enter the 6-digit code sent to your email or authenticator app.
  4. Check "Remember this device for 30 days" so you won't be repeatedly asked for codes on your private computer.
Go to Sign In
3

How to Reset a Forgotten Password

  1. On the Sign In page, click Forgot password?.
  2. Enter your registered email address and click Send recovery link.
  3. Open the recovery link from your email (or the Dev Outbox).
  4. Choose a strong new password (we recommend a 4-word passphrase) and save.
Open Password Recovery
4

Using Accessibility & Guided Mode

  • Guided Switch: Click Guided in the top navigation bar to turn on extra tips, simplified layouts, and larger input fields.
  • A- / A / A+: Adjusts font size dynamically without distorting the layout.
  • Read Aloud: Press the speaker icon in the top bar to hear page instructions read aloud.
  • High Contrast (HC): Select the HC button for maximum text visibility and clear borders.

Common Errors & How to Fix Them

AuthEase displays plain English explanations for every situation. Here are the most common reason codes and the exact steps to resolve them:

EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED "Email address not yet confirmed"
Registration

Why it happens: You completed registration, but the account has not been activated via the email link yet.

💡 Steps to fix:
  1. Go to the Simulated Dev Outbox.
  2. Find your email and click the green button: [👉 Verify & Activate Account Now].
  3. Or click "Resend confirmation email" on the Check Email page.
INVALID_CREDENTIALS "Email or password does not match"
Login

Why it happens: The email address was not found, or the password entered does not match your account.

💡 Steps to fix:
  1. Double check for typing mistakes and ensure Caps Lock is OFF.
  2. If you forgot your password, click Forgot password? to set a new one.
  3. If you haven't created an account yet, create one here.
TOKEN_INVALID_OR_EXPIRED "Link expired or already used"
Security Links

Why it happens: Verification and password reset links are single-use and expire for your security (verification links expire in 24 hours; approval links in 10 minutes).

💡 Steps to fix:
  1. Single-use links cannot be clicked twice. If you already verified, proceed directly to Log in.
  2. If the link expired, request a fresh one using "Resend verification" or "Forgot password".
PASSWORD_BREACHED "Password found in public breach leaks"
NIST Security

Why it happens: AuthEase checks NIST SP 800-63B compromise blocklists. The password entered is one of the top stolen passwords used by attackers (e.g. password123, admin123).

💡 Steps to fix:

Choose a longer passphrase made of 3 to 4 random words (e.g., correct-horse-battery-staple). You do not need to memorize weird symbols or numbers!

OTP_INVALID / OTP_EXPIRED "Incorrect or expired 6-digit code"
Two-Factor

Why it happens: A typo occurred when entering the 6-digit code, or the 5-minute time window expired before the code was submitted.

💡 Steps to fix:
  1. Check the latest code in your authenticator app (codes change every 30 seconds).
  2. If using Email OTP, check the latest message in the Dev Outbox.
  3. Click "Send me a new code" to generate a fresh 6-digit code.
RATE_DELAY "Too many attempts in a short time"
Rate Limiting

Why it happens: Repeated rapid sign-in attempts triggered progressive backoff protection to stop automated brute-force attacks.

💡 Steps to fix:

Wait for the countdown timer displayed on screen to reach zero (usually 2 to 30 seconds), then submit your credentials once more.

CHALLENGE_LOCKED "Security check locked after 5 failed tries"
Lockout Defense

Why it happens: An incorrect 2FA code was entered 5 times consecutively. The active challenge is locked to protect the account from guessing.

💡 Steps to fix:

Return to the Log in page and start fresh by re-entering your email and password.
