What is Guided Mode?

Guided Mode simplifies forms, enlarges fonts, and provides step-by-step tips for each input field. You can turn it on or off at any time using the Guided switch in the top toolbar.

How does Adaptive Security work?

When you sign in from your familiar browser, you are logged in instantly. If you log in from a new computer or location, AuthEase asks for a 6-digit code to confirm it's really you. If someone tries to sign in from an unfamiliar country or with repeated bad passwords, an approval email is sent to protect your account.

Where do I find my verification emails in this demo?

All verification emails and codes are delivered directly to the Simulated Dev Outbox. You can open and click them with a single click.
